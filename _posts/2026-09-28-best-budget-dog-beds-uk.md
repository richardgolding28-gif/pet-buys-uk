Dog beds fail in one of two ways: the filling flattens into nothing within weeks, or the cover isn't actually removable so it just gets grim and stays that way. Below is what to check for, and which ones hold up.

## What actually matters

- **A genuinely removable, machine-washable cover** — not just "wipeable". Dogs shed, drool, and track mud; you'll be washing this a lot.
- **Anti-slip base** — matters on laminate or tile floors, where a bed without grip ends up bunched in a corner within a day.
- **Filling that holds its shape** — cheap polyester filling flattens fast; look for reviews mentioning it after a few months, not just out of the box.
- **Realistic sizing** — measure your dog stretched out, not curled up, and size up from that.
