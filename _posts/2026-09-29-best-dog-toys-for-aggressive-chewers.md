- question: Is any dog toy actually "indestructible"?
  answer: No toy is completely indestructible against a determined power chewer, and any manufacturer claiming otherwise is overselling it. - question: How do I know if my dog is an "aggressive chewer"?
  answer: If your dog regularly destroys standard rubber or plush toys within a single session, or you've found pieces missing that they may have swallowed, treat them as a power chewer and buy accordingly. Breed and jaw strength matter, but individual dogs within the same breed vary a lot.

A toy that lasts an afternoon with a determined chewer isn't just a waste of money — bits of shredded rubber or plush end up swallowed. Here's what actually holds up, and which toys are worth the higher price tag.

## What actually matters

- **Solid, dense rubber or nylon construction** — thin or hollow-walled toys that feel light for their size are usually the first to get punctured.
- **Size matched to your dog**, not just their breed — an undersized toy is both a choking risk and easier to actually crush with the back teeth.
- **Non-toxic, food-grade materials** — check this explicitly, since intense chewing means more material actually ends up ingested over time.
- **A shape your dog will keep engaging with** — treat-dispensing toys and irregular bounce shapes keep interest longer than a toy that just sits there once the novelty wears off.
