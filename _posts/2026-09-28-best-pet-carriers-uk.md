A carrier that's flimsy at the zips or too small for your pet to turn around in makes every vet visit or trip worse than it needs to be. Here's what to check, and which ones hold up.

## What actually matters

- **Secure, self-locking zips** — a carrier a determined cat can push open from inside is worse than useless.
- **Enough room to turn around and lie down** — not just enough for your pet to fit in stationary.
- **A washable interior** — accidents happen, especially with nervous animals, and a carrier you can't clean properly stays smelling long after.
- **How it's actually carried** — shoulder strap, handle, or backpack style all suit different situations; car travel and airport walks have different practical needs.
